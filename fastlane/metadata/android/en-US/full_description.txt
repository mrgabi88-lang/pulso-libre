Pulso Libre helps you discover events, workshops, fairs and community gatherings.

Browse each event's description, date, location, flyers and optional audio. Events may offer free reservations with individual QR tickets, open admission without reservations, or organizer-managed payments with receipt review.

Create an account and confirm your email to manage tickets. For paid events, follow the organizer's transfer instructions and upload a receipt. Tickets are enabled after the organizer approves the payment. Rejected receipts can be corrected within the same purchase.

Open Tickets to view your admissions and show the current dynamic QR at the entrance. Ticket transfers are available for eligible tickets. Admission requires the organizer's server-confirmed validation; the app does not offer offline admission guarantees.

Organizers create events and review receipts through the web panel. PLLABS does not charge commissions for using Pulso Libre; an event's ticket price is set by its organizer.

This client requires the PLLABS server. It does not offer a user-selectable server. Organizer-selected SoundCloud tracks may load a third-party web player. Account and event services run on a backend that is not part of this source release.
